Strategic Business Recommendations for Leadership & Sales Teams

Volume Growth

1. Capitalize on High-Volume States

States like Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra generate ~32% of total national volume. Ensure distributor inventory stock is replenished before peak Wednesday-Thursday scan surges.

Retailer Intensity

2. Replicate High-Intensity Best Practices

Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu exhibit the highest average scans per retailer (>40 scans/retailer). Analyze distributor engagement in these markets to adopt best practices across underperforming states.

Packaging Shift

3. Drive Box (B5) Packaging Adoption

Box packaging (B5) represents 58.2% of scans. Introducing an extra loyalty reward point for Box scans in categories like Sporto Neo can accelerate bulk trade adoption over individual pieces.

Retention & Loyalty

4. Nurture Bronze & Silver Retailer Tiers

Over 45% of retailers scan between 5-20 times per month. Targeted mid-month bonus gamification can nudge these occasional scanners into Gold-tier (>50 scans) power contributors.

Hourly Optimization

5. Optimize Engagement Timing

By analyzing Hourly Scan Activity, marketing teams can deploy SMS/WhatsApp promotional nudges 1-2 hours before the typical peak scanning windows to maximize engagement.

Tracking Bronze Retailers out of Total Retailers

Distribution Overview

What is "Bronze Upgrade Target"?

These retailers scan between 5 to 20 times per month (Bronze tier). If each one scans just a few more times to reach 21 scans, they upgrade to Silver tier — meaning more consistent business. The "Scans to Silver" column shows exactly how many more scans each retailer needs.

Almost There (1-3 needed): Just a nudge — send a reminder SMS
Close (4-8 needed): Offer a small incentive to push them
Needs Work (9-16 needed): Requires sales team engagement

What do the Consistency Tiers mean?

This measures total volume & engagement tiers of each retailer based on number of scans completed across July 2026.
